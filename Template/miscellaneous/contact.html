Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password.

How do I list an item for sale?

Navigate to the Marketplace section, click "Sell Item", fill in the product details including title, description, price, and upload photos. Once reviewed, your listing will be visible to other students.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and mobile money options like M-PESA depending on your region. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

How do I join a study group?

Browse the Groups section, find a group that matches your interests, and click "Join Group". Some groups may require admin approval before you can participate.

Is Campus Connect free to use?

Basic features including browsing, joining groups, and asking questions are completely free. Some premium features and marketplace transactions may have associated fees, which are clearly disclosed.

How do I report inappropriate content?

Use the "Report" button available on posts, listings, and user profiles. Our moderation team will review the report and take appropriate action within 24 hours.
