01

Introduction

Overview of our privacy commitment

Welcome to Campus Connect's Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, and services. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This policy applies to all users of Campus Connect, including students, educators, administrators, and visitors. Please read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data.

By using Campus Connect, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our services immediately.
02

Information We Collect

Types of data we gather from you

We collect several types of information from and about users of our platform, including:

Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, student ID, phone number, institution name, and graduation year.
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, bio, academic interests, courses, and study preferences.
  • Transaction Information: Payment details, purchase history, and marketplace listings.
  • Communication Data: Messages, comments, posts, and feedback you submit on the platform.
  • Verification Data: Student ID cards, institutional email, or other verification documents.

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, features used, search queries, and interaction patterns.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on IP address.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Information about your browsing behavior and preferences.
Sensitive Information: We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as government IDs, financial account numbers, or health data. Please do not share such information on public areas of the platform.
03

How We Use Your Information

Purposes for processing your data

We use your personal information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain Services: Create and manage your account, facilitate marketplace transactions, enable group participation, and deliver requested features.
  • To Improve and Optimize: Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience, develop new features, and improve platform performance.
  • To Communicate: Send important notifications, updates, security alerts, and respond to your inquiries.
  • To Ensure Safety and Security: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other security issues.
  • To Personalize Experience: Recommend relevant content, groups, and products based on your interests.
  • To Comply with Legal Obligations: Respond to lawful requests from authorities and fulfill regulatory requirements.
We will never sell your personal information to third parties. Any data sharing is done solely for the purpose of providing and improving our services.
04

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

How we use cookies and similar technologies

Campus Connect uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze platform usage.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for platform functionality, authentication, and security.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with our platform.

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings and preferences.

Your Cookie Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.

By continuing to use Campus Connect without changing your cookie settings, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.
05

Data Sharing & Disclosure

When and why we share your information

We may share your personal information in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you direct us to share your information with third parties.
  • Service Providers: With vendors who perform services on our behalf (payment processing, cloud hosting, analytics, customer support).
  • Within Your Institution: With your educational institution for verification and compliance purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect the safety, rights, or property of Campus Connect, our users, or the public.
We require all third-party service providers to maintain appropriate security measures and use your information only for specified purposes consistent with this Privacy Policy.
06

Data Security

How we protect your information

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security Measures Include

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data transmission
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Access controls and authentication protocols
  • Secure data storage with encryption at rest
  • Employee security training and confidentiality agreements
  • Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures
While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication where available.
07

Your Privacy Rights

Control over your personal information

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access

Request a copy of your personal data

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal data

Right to Object

Object to certain data processing

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a portable format

Right to Restrict

Limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@campusconnect.edu. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations may provide additional rights depending on your jurisdiction. We are committed to honoring these rights to the fullest extent.
08

Data Retention

How long we keep your information

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Retention Periods

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter.
  • Marketplace Listings: Transaction records retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance.
  • Communication Logs: Messages and posts retained for up to 3 years after account deletion.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated usage data retained indefinitely for platform improvement.

After your account is deleted, we will anonymize or delete your personal information, except where we are required to retain data for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes.

09

Children's Privacy

Our commitment to protecting minors

Campus Connect is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

For users aged 13-18, we require parental or guardian consent where required by applicable law. We encourage parents to monitor their children's online activities and discuss the importance of protecting personal information.

If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information and terminate the associated account.
10

International Data Transfers

Cross-border data processing

Campus Connect operates globally, and your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. These countries may have data protection laws different from your jurisdiction.

We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by relevant authorities
  • Binding Corporate Rules for intra-company transfers
  • Data processing agreements with service providers
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable
By using Campus Connect, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries where our servers and service providers are located, including the United States.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

How we notify you of updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. We will notify you of material changes through:

  • A prominent notice on our website and mobile application
  • Email notification to the address associated with your account
  • In-app notifications and banners

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when changes were made. Your continued use of Campus Connect after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
