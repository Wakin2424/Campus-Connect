Our Story

Born in a dorm room.
Built for every student.

The story of how frustration became a platform.

Campus Connect was born out of a simple frustration. In 2022, a group of university students were tired of hunting through scattered group chats to sell old textbooks, missing study group announcements buried in email threads, and having no central place to access shared notes. So they built one.

What started as a small internal tool for three friends quickly spread across their campus. Within weeks, hundreds of students were signing up. The problem was universal — every student on every campus was dealing with the same fragmented, inefficient campus life experience.

Campus Connect solves this by bringing everything together: a peer marketplace for textbooks and supplies, organized study groups, collaborative note-sharing, and a vibrant community — all under one roof. No more chaos. Just seamless student life.

Today, we serve over 50,000 students across 120+ campuses — and we're just getting started.
