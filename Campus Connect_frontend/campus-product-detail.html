Product Description

Introduction to Algorithms is a comprehensive textbook covering the full spectrum of modern algorithms. This third edition has been revised and updated throughout, with new chapters on van Emde Boas trees and multithreaded algorithms, substantial additions to the chapter on recurrence, and an appendix on matrices.


It features improved treatment of dynamic programming and greedy algorithms and a new notion of edge-based flow in the material on flow networks. Many new exercises and problems have been added for this edition. As of the third edition, this textbook is published exclusively by the MIT Press.


Key Features:

  • Comprehensive coverage of elementary data structures
  • Advanced data structures including B-trees, Fibonacci heaps
  • Graph algorithms and their applications
  • Mathematical foundations and analysis of algorithms
  • Hundreds of practice problems and exercises
  • Real-world applications and case studies

This book is in excellent condition with minimal wear. All pages are intact with no highlighting or writing. Perfect for students taking algorithms courses or anyone looking to strengthen their understanding of fundamental computer science concepts.
