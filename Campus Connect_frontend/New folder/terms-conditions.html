Campus Connect's community features are designed to foster meaningful connections and academic collaboration. All users engaging in groups, forums, or public spaces must abide by the following community standards:

  • Respect & Inclusion: Treat every member with dignity and respect, regardless of background, ethnicity, gender, religion, or viewpoint.
  • Relevance: Keep all posts and discussions relevant to the group's stated purpose and academic focus.
  • No Spam or Solicitation: Do not post repetitive, unsolicited, or commercially motivated content unrelated to the group.
  • Zero Harassment: Bullying, threatening, stalking, or harassing any member in any form is strictly forbidden and may be reported to university authorities.
