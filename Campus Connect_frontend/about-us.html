Our Story

Born in a dorm room.
Built for every student.

The story of how frustration became a platform.

Campus Connect was born out of a simple frustration. In 2022, a group of university students were tired of hunting through scattered group chats to sell old textbooks, missing study group announcements buried in email threads, and having no central place to access shared notes. So they built one.

What started as a small internal tool for three friends quickly spread across their campus. Within weeks, hundreds of students were signing up. The problem was universal — every student on every campus was dealing with the same fragmented, inefficient campus life experience.

Campus Connect solves this by bringing everything together: a peer marketplace for textbooks and supplies, organized study groups, collaborative note-sharing, and a vibrant community — all under one roof. No more chaos. Just seamless student life.

Today, we serve over 50,000 students across 120+ campuses — and we're just getting started. Every feature we build starts with the same question: What do students actually need?

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Every decision we make is grounded in our mission and guided by our vision for the future of student life.

Our Mission

Empower Every Student

To create an inclusive, safe, and empowering digital campus environment where every student — regardless of background — has equal access to academic resources, community connections, and peer-to-peer opportunities that make campus life richer and more successful.

Our Vision

The Future of Campus Life

To become the world's most trusted student super-platform — a single destination where every aspect of academic and campus life is simplified, connected, and supercharged. We envision a future where no student feels isolated, uninformed, or unsupported.

By 2030, we aim to serve 1 million students across 500+ universities globally, redefining what it means to belong to a campus community.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four principles that guide every feature we build, every policy we write, and every decision we make.

Community

We believe students thrive when they feel a genuine sense of belonging. Every feature we build strengthens bonds between students, creating a campus community that extends beyond physical boundaries.

Innovation

Student life is constantly evolving, and so are we. We continuously reinvent our platform with fresh ideas, cutting-edge technology, and student-first thinking to stay ahead of what campus life demands.

Integrity

Trust is the cornerstone of any student community. We operate with complete transparency, enforce fair policies, and hold ourselves accountable to the same standards we expect from our users.

Collaboration

Great things happen when students work together. We break down silos, bridge gaps between departments, and create tools that make peer collaboration — in study groups or the marketplace — effortless.
